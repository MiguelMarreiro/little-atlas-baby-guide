Before paying for a used set

Ask for photos of the frame and carrycot labels, both adapter pairs, and any replacement parts. At collection, fit and remove the cot, test both locks and the harness, check the brake, and roll the stroller with the front wheel locked and unlocked. Check the air tyres and wheel attachments. Obtain a repair estimate for worn items before agreeing the price. Which? inspection guide · UK recalls · EU recalls

Buy the priced car seat and base new. The OLX seat has no verified identity or crash history. For a known-history used base, establish the exact model, instructions, completeness, service life and history with CYBEX, and confirm fit in the intended vehicle. ADAC’s cited test applies specifically to Cloud T with Base T. NHS car seats · Portuguese child safety guidance · Base T UK · ADAC test
